Donate

Wesh is free and published on a non-profit basis. No ads, no subscription, no paid version, and no data to sell: the app sends us nothing.

Support Wesh

The project runs on donations. They fund its development and maintenance.

Donate on Tipeee

  • The link opens the Tipeee website, which handles payment under its own privacy rules.
  • The app contains no payment and no in-app purchase.
  • Donating unlocks nothing: everyone gets the same app.

Other ways to help

  • Contribute code, report a bug or offer a translation on iOS or Android.
  • Report a flaw responsibly: see Security.
  • Install Wesh with the people close to you and swap QR codes ahead of time.

Wesh is free and open-source software, published on a non-profit basis by Samouraï Coop, a French cooperative and open-source engineering studio.
