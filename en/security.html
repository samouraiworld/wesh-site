Report a vulnerability

Found a flaw in Wesh? Write to us privately before publishing anything. Wesh is used when networks are down: a flaw can then hurt the people who depend on it.

Contact

Address
security@samourai.coop

The security.txt file carries the same information.

What to send us

  • Platform and versions: app, operating system, phone model.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, and its impact.
  • A proof of concept, if you have one.
  • No data belonging to other people.

What we ask of you

  • Test on your own devices, or with the explicit consent of the people involved.
  • Do not disrupt real use of Wesh and do not read other people's messages.
  • Give us time to fix the issue before publishing.

What we commit to

  • Acknowledge your report and keep you informed of the fix.
  • Agree on a publication date with you.
  • Thank you publicly, if you wish.

Scope

In scope

  • The Wesh app for iOS (wesh-bitchat-ios) and Android (wesh-bitchat-android), released versions and main branch.
  • The protocol as implemented by Wesh: Bluetooth mesh, private message encryption, QR code verification, Internet mode over Tor.
  • Encrypted storage on the phone and emergency erase.
  • APK files distributed on this site.
  • This website.

Out of scope

  • The BitChat app and its repositories: report to the BitChat project, and tell us if Wesh is affected too.
  • Third-party Nostr relays and the Tor network: report to their operators or to the Tor Project.
  • Flaws in iOS and Android or their Bluetooth stacks.
  • Radio jamming and denial of service by flooding Bluetooth.
  • Documented limits: the local room is public, the phone is detectable over Bluetooth, public keys stay stable until reset, relays see metadata. Ideas to reduce them are welcome.
