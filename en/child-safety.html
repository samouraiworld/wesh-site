Child safety

Wesh standards — 25 October 2026. Publisher: Samouraï Coop.

Wesh prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), in the public room and in private conversations. The app is for people aged 18 and over; this age limit does not replace these protections.

Neither the current app nor this website verifies age. Store restrictions do not guarantee the absence of minors among participants in the decentralised network, including earlier or outside-store installations and third-party BitChat clients.

Zero tolerance

Grooming, sextortion, child sex trafficking, and creating, requesting, selling or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM) are prohibited. This includes artificially generated depictions. The text-only version of Wesh does not permit any of these uses.

Report without redistributing

In Wesh, choose Report on a message or person: this control blocks the person locally and prepares an email that you review before choosing to send it. You can also write to support@samourai.coop, our child safety contact.

Briefly describe the concern and limit personal information to what is necessary. Do not download, copy or attach child sexual abuse imagery. Do not send keys, unrelated conversations or information that could locate a person. Email is not end-to-end encrypted.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your country's emergency services (112 in the European Union). Our support inbox is not an emergency service.

Responding to reports

We prioritise reports of children at risk. When CSAM is confirmed, we report it to the competent authorities under applicable law and remove any illegal material under our control. We do not request another copy of this material. Information needed to handle the report is limited and treated according to our privacy policy.

What we can do

Wesh has no central messaging server. We cannot read private messages, delete copies on other people's phones, recall a relayed message or ban an identity from the entire BitChat network. Blocking and hiding take effect on your phone. We assess necessary app changes after a report; we do not promise global removal or monitoring of private conversations.
